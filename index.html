The Bio

MULTIPLE VOICES.
ONE MOVEMENT.

The Unit is a collective of rappers who came together to create something bigger than any one of us. We're building more than a fanbase, we're building a movement. A place where people can find community and stand in solidarity with each other.

Composed of rappers sakesa (formerly known as aj1kk), EERIE, qreekk, AIP, and DJ wonder. Every member brings their own voice, their own style, and their own story, but we all move with the same purpose. That's what makes us a unit and not just a group.

When you're with The Unit, you're not just listening to the music — you're part of the movement.
